SCREEN BRIDGE

A second screen, from a browser

Give your Mac a little more room.

Screen Bridge connects your Mac to a nearby tablet or computer and turns its browser into an extended display. Choose Wi-Fi or cable mode, pair your device, and set the display quality that works for you.
