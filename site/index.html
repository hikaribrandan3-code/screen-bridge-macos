SCREEN BRIDGE
Experimental · Apple silicon
A second screen, from a browser

Give your Mac a little more room.

Screen Bridge experiments with using a nearby tablet browser as an extended Mac display. I have used it successfully, with noticeable lag and incomplete reliability. Pair over a trusted local network and try the display quality that works for you.

Pair simply

Use the app’s device selector and on-screen pairing flow to connect on your local network.

Choose your connection

Select Wi-Fi or cable mode and tune resolution and image quality in the app.

Use a browser

The companion display runs in a modern browser on a tablet or another computer.

Experimental prototype. Version 0.1.1 source is available; its updated build awaits hands-on smoke testing. The linked 0.1.0 ZIP predates the latest fixes. The app declares macOS 12 or later; audio capture needs macOS 14.2 or later. Local streaming is unencrypted. Use a trusted network, and expect compatibility and performance to vary.
